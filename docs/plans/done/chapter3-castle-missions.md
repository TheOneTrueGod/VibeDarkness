## Plan-level decisions (locked)

### Brick architecture: terrain variants

- **Brick is a new enum value with a variant link, not a per-cell side layer.** Add `TerrainType.Brick = 9` (append it and never renumber, because checkpoints store the numbers). Add two optional fields to `TerrainProperties`:
  - `variantOf?: TerrainType`: the base type it behaves as (Brick → Rock). Leave it out for base types.
  - `access?: TerrainAccessId`: the capability a unit needs to *mutate or draw on* this terrain (Brick → `'brick'`). Leave it out when there is no gate. `TerrainAccessId` is a string union in `TerrainType.ts`; it starts as `'brick'`.
- **Brick's properties copy Rock's** (passable, blocks projectiles/vision/light, `hard-edge`, `blocksBleed`). It has its own `color` and `name`. It is in the same hard-edge family as Rock and Gravel in both directions: Rock's, Gravel's and Brick's `hardEdgeFamily` lists all include each other, so the seams draw clean.
- **Two helpers in `TerrainType.ts`, used at every rock-comparing site:**
  - `baseTerrainOf(type)` returns `variantOf ?? type`. Use it for *what the terrain is* questions: collision lists (`collidesWithTerrain: [Rock]` matches Brick), `ROCK_AND_WATER_TERRAIN` blocking, preview priority, and rock-like render paths.
  - `canUnitAffectTerrain(type, access: ReadonlySet<TerrainAccessId> | null)` returns true when the type has no `access`, or when the set contains it. Use it for *may this actor change it or draw on it* questions: `damageRock*`, `isOnStone` / `isUnitOnStone`, Digging Claws' `terrainCondition`, Rock Slide, Gather Stone, Shaking Ground, `rockHitsToBreak`, stonephase traverse, and Force Push wall checks.
- **Without access, Brick is indestructible rock.** Damage does nothing (it returns null, the same as hitting non-rock). It does not count as stone. Collision, blocking and light still behave exactly as Rock. **A mutation with no source unit is refused on gated terrain.**
- **With access, Brick behaves the same as Rock**: the same health (`EARTH_CORE_STONE_HEALTH`), crack tiers, and breaking into Gravel. `createOrMarkRock` on intact Brick re-marks it as Brick, keeping the type.
- **Capability hook only, with no research node.** `Unit.terrainAccess: Set<TerrainAccessId>` is an instance field. It is serialized, defaults to empty, and is not on the def, because research will grant it before battle later (see the `game-object-def-pattern` skill). Add `grantTerrainAccess(id)` and `hasTerrainAccess(id)`. TerrainManager mutation APIs that take a `sourceUnitId` look the unit up through a resolver that the engine wires (in the same way `setTerrainLayers` is wired). The tiny-battle harness and the AbilityTest scenario builder gain a `terrainAccess` option. No `ResearchEffect` is added.
- **Future variants fit without new gameplay checks.** A Flowers variant would be `variantOf: Grass` with no `access`, and abilities would read `type === Flowers` for their special interactions. A River variant would be `variantOf: ShallowWater` plus a future push property. Write this down in the `terrain/AGENTS.md` "Terrain variants" section. Do not build either one.
- **Look:** `HardEdgeLayerRenderer` draws Brick with Rock's fill, chamfer and border, then adds a running-bond mortar overlay. That is horizontal course lines every ⅓ of a cell, with vertical joints offset by half a brick on alternate courses, slightly darker than the fill and clipped to the cell core. Crack overlays (`FloorTileRenderer`) apply to Brick the same as to Rock. The terrain editor, segment previews (priority equal to Rock's) and ascii (`'B'` / `'B'`) all gain an entry.

### Castle layout after the rework

```
         58          59             60             61              62            63            64
  43  NW tower    N wall walk    N stair tower  Royal treasury* [Tower         Tower      ]  NE tower
  44  wall walk~  Master bedroom Solar/dressing THRONE ROOM     [ library      alchemy lab]  wall walk~
  45  wall walk~  Royal baths    Privy chamber  Chasm bridge    [ study        great circle] wall walk~
  46  Sally port~ Portcullis hall Barracks(out) Great hall      Chapel nave(out) Chapel altar E breach~

  48  wall walk   Kennel yard    Sparring pit   Courtyard       [Herb garden   Orchard    ]  wall walk
  49  SW tower    Storerooms     W guardhouse   Gate            E guardhouse   Ice house     SE tower
```
`~` marks wall-walk tiles that the winding-wall missions run through. `*` means behind the throne, reached by its secret door. Rows 47-48 (cols 59-63) are **outdoors**: grass, water and walking paths, with little rock. Row 46 is the **transition** to indoors: the chapel and barracks are outbuildings with yard around them, and the great hall is the door into the keep.

- **The garden and arcane 2x2s swap places.** Each keeps its internal layout (a pond loop or the summoning-circle loop). Files and ids are renamed to their new address. The garden's edges are then repainted to meet the openings around it: the courtyard's new east door, the crossroads, and the E breach side.
- **The courtyard (61_48)** keeps its south gate and gains wide doorways (4 or more tiles) to the west (the training ring) and east (the garden). Its north exit leads to the 61_47 crossroads.
- **Training ring (59-60 x 47-48)** is one open outdoor ring. It works in archery targets (rock or brick posts with gravel butts), scattered kennels (small brick huts), a sparring pit (a dirt ring) and a training yard. Its only way in is from the courtyard's west door, so the area is optional.
- **Throne room (61_44)** loses its west and east side doors. The chasm bridge (61_45) from the great hall is its only entrance; the secret door to the treasury stays.
- **Royal chambers (59-60 x 44-45)** become one 2x2 of rooms (master bedroom, baths, solar or dressing room, privy chamber) joined by 1-2-wide doors. They are reached only through the west wall walk (58_44 / 58_45), not from the great hall wing or the throne room.
- **Wizard tower (62-63 x 44-45)** is reached only through the east wall walk (64_44 / 64_45) from the E breach (64_46), and not from the throne room or the garden.
- **The outer ring has fewer openings.** Most wall-walk tiles no longer open inward. Openings stay at the gate, the sally port to the portcullis hall, the breach to the chapel, one wall-walk door each into the royal chambers and the tower, and the two guardhouses or storerooms used by quests. The two wall walks used by missions wind (they double back through guardrooms and stairs) but stay at least 2 wide.
- **Brick pass:** most castle Rock becomes Brick. Keep some natural Rock and Gravel: rubble piles, collapsed wall sections, the chasm edges and outcrops in the outdoor band. Aim for roughly 10-15% of the stone cells left as Rock, spread through every quarter, so Earth Core always has something to work with. The gate and courtyard (61_49 and 61_48) get the same pass.
- **Tooling:** if no such helper exists, add `terrain/mapTools/segmentBlock.ts` with two functions. `stitchSegmentBlock(byAddress, col0, row0, cols, rows)` turns segments into one canvas. `sliceSegmentBlock(canvas, …)` turns the canvas back into per-address grids. Workers repaint on the stitched canvas with mapTools, slice it, then rewrite the segment `.ts` files and regenerate each `.svg` with `generateSegmentPreviewSvg`. `castle.test.ts` switches to the shared stitch helper. **No segment edits through the terrain editor's storage JSON.** If storage holds edits for these segments, note it in the step summary and stop.
