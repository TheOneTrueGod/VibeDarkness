His kit asks for each mechanic. Notes only, nothing decided:

- **Reactions**: attacks that are dangerous if finished, Reactable, so the party can spend Reflex to pause and answer them at the reaction frame (see [the Garden Heart](plan:0008)). Crystal Laser would be Reactable too, which stays inert until the party has learned the mechanic.
- **Time dilation** (see [the royal quarters boss](plan:0009)): the one still without a use. It is the mechanic least tied to a single fight, so this boss should make it clear what the party does with it.
