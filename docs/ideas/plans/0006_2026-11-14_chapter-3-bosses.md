# Chapter 3

Chapter 3 has four boss fights, each in a different part of the castle, and each one teaches the party a mechanic they will need for the last. The final fight, in the throne room, is against **The Fallen King**. His abilities are built around three mechanics: super attacks, Reactions (stopping an attack midway) and time dilation. The requirement is soft: the party can in principle win without them, but will most likely not have the raw power to. This plan holds the arc and the order of work. Each boss has its own plan, linked below. See also [Chapter 3 Preparations](plan:0001).

---

## Summary {section}

**The arc.** Each boss introduces one mechanic in a fight that is cheap to learn it in, then the Fallen King asks for all three at once.

- **Super attacks**: high burst damage, needed to break through a regenerating shield. Introduced by [the Court Magician](plan:0007).
- **Reactions**: a limited resource, Reflex, that lets a player pause a Reactable boss attack at its reaction frame and answer it. Introduced by [the Garden Heart](plan:0008).
- **Time dilation**: not yet fitted in. Introduced by [the royal quarters boss](plan:0009).

**The four fights, in the order they are being planned.**

- **[The Court Magician](plan:0007)**: the wizard's tower (`court_magician_tower`, summoning circle). Built. Introduces super attacks.
- **[The Garden Heart](plan:0008)**: the mission `garden_heart` (queen's garden and conservatory). A living plant whose Heart is shelled while anything grows from it. Introduces Reactions. Designed, with an artbook.
- **[The royal quarters boss](plan:0009)**: the Royal Bedchamber (`royal_bedchamber`, master bedroom, solar, baths and privy chamber). Introduces time dilation. Not started.
- **[The Fallen King](plan:0010)**: the throne room (`the_kings_court`, segment `61_44_throne`). The final boss. Needs all three.

**What exists today.** The Garden Heart, Royal Bedchamber and King's Court missions are all placeholders: an Alpha Wolf plus the `castle_boss` escort (`missions/016_the_kings_court.ts`, `020_garden_heart.ts`, `028_royal_bedchamber.ts`). Only the Court Magician has a real setup (`castle/castleBosses.ts`). All four missions are built with `defineCastleMission`, so a new boss is a `boss: { kind: 'custom', ... }` setup, not a new mission class.

**Working rule for The Fallen King.** Each mechanic should make a part of his kit tractable rather than being a hard gate. Brute force stays possible, just very expensive.

## Suggested order {section}

- **First**: finish [the Court Magician](plan:0007) by answering whether it needs a player super.
- **Then**: [the Garden Heart](plan:0008). Settle the co-op Reaction claim rule first, because every other Garden Heart design choice depends on them.
- **Then**: [the royal quarters boss](plan:0009), which settles what time dilation does.
- **Last**: [The Fallen King](plan:0010), once all three mechanics have a working example.
