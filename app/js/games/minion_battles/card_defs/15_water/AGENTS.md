# Water (Ability group 15)

Water is its own skill tree. Its cards spend the **Water** resource (`resources/Water.ts`), which a caster draws from nearby water tiles, draining them a level at a time. While drawing, each tile shows a ripple and a small circular progress ring for how much of its level is gone, with droplets streaming into the caster: `game/GameRenderer/renderers/TerrainSiphonRenderer.ts` (maths in `siphonVisual.ts`). It reads the render-only `Resource.siphonTiles`, which `tickTerrainSiphon` fills, so any siphon resource gets it in its own colours. The first slice overlaps the Blood Mage; a standalone Water core comes later.

**Visual identity:** Tidewater (`WATER_THEME` in `themes/abilityThemes.ts`): deep navy for mass (ice bodies, the field, the research border), seafoam for the event (edges, frost trails, splashes, the resource). Every card is an **ice** card: frost crystals, shards and droplets. Long windups draw the `water` spell circle (`SPELL_CIRCLE_STYLE_WATER` in `game/spellCircles/spellCircles.ts`): a navy ring with turning seafoam rune dashes, shedding ice shards and falling droplets. The look was chosen in the Ideas → Plans artbook `docs/ideas/plans/0005_2026-11-11_water-tree.md`, whose studio art (`components/minionBattlesHomePage/Ideas/fxArt/waterArt.tsx`) is the visual spec.

**Mechanical feel:** long, committed casts for big effects. The caster stands still through the windup and is paid back with a large area or a strong hit.

**The Blood Mage overlap:** each ice card **replaces** its blood card in prep (research `replaceCard`), so the player picks one card per pair. The slot has a **Blood | Ice mode strip** (`abilityModes`, `toggleStyle: 'cycle'`; all three cards list `blood` first and label the modes "Blood" and "Ice"). With no pick, `fallbackMode: 'blood'` makes the slot show and cast the blood card whenever Ice can't be cast (not enough Water, no uses); the player can still pick Ice to see it (disabled) and why, and `ownedCardModes: ['blood']` keeps the Blood mode only for a character that owned the blood card before the replacement (`Unit.lockedAbilityModes`). With only the ice card there is no strip, and the card greys out when it can't be paid for. `resolveEffectiveMode` (`abilities/resolveAbilityMode.ts`) decides the mode for the UI, order submission and the cast; no engine code names Water or a card id. The equipped core owns the Water bar through `ItemDef.resourceGrants` (Blood Mage core: max 10, starting 6); Frozen Field costs a flat 10. See `abilities/AGENTS.md` and `card_defs/03_blood_mage/AGENTS.md`.
