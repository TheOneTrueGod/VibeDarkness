# Earth Core (Ability group **05**)

This folder is **`05_earth_core`**: all player-facing Earth skill cards use **4-digit ids starting with `05`** (`AbilityGroupId.Earth` in `card_defs/AbilityGroupId.ts`). The narrative name is “Earth Core” or “earth skills.”

For long-form design history and future slices, see `Plans/plan.md` (Earth Core Skill Tree).

## Player profile

Earth Core is for players who want to **own space through terrain** and **convert defense into pressure or support**. The fantasy is reading the battlefield through stone, shaping rock, and riding a separate pacing layer (Resonance) that rewards fighting *on* and *through* stone. It fits both **ranged control** and **melee bruiser / ally support** without forcing a single playstyle: you can lean artillery and zone denial, or brawl and prop up the team with armour and strips.

## Two build lanes + shared spine

The tree is intentionally **split** so both fantasies share one identity:

1. **Shared / core lane** (both builds): baseline **Tremorsense**-style awareness, the **Resonance** rules, and **Rock Armour**-style defense—so every Earth user “speaks stone” the same way.
2. **Ranged / Tremors lane**: **Fault Harvest**, **Stone Tomb**, **Knock** (with **Stonephase**), **Seismic Guard**, **Anchored Tremor**, **Stoney Punch**—terrain creation, line play through rock, and area pressure.
3. **Melee / bruiser–support lane**: **Impact Conversion**, **Bedrock Scavenger**, **Deep Resonance**, **Shaking Ground**, **Earthern Punch**, **Shatter**—sustain, resonance from contact and armour events, and burst that cares about stone.

Abilities are generally **usable off stone** but **stronger on or through stone**; that is the main expression of the “stone synergy” rule in card text and implementation.

## Mechanics in play

| Theme | Role in Earth Core |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Tremorsense** | Reveal and interact with enemies in a **shared diameter** (tuning: `EARTH_CORE_SHARED_DIAMETER` in `earthCoreConstants.ts`). Helpers in `abilities/earthCoreHelpers.ts` centralize “on stone” and distance checks. |
| **Stone / terrain** | Earth uses **stone tile states** (`natural_stone`, `created_rock`, `cracked_rock`; not `spent_rubble`) for “on stone” and events. **Stone durability** and damage per instance are tuned in `earthCoreConstants.ts` and applied from terrain code (e.g. `TerrainManager`, `TerrainGrid`). |
| **Stonephase** | **Projectiles** can be granted rules so travel **inside rock** does not eat range / durability the same way as in air (see **Knock** and projectile pipeline). |
| **Armour (Rock / Earth)** | Multiple abilities grant or spend armour; some paths use **per-source caps and diminishing returns** (see below). **Armour break** and **armour lost to damage** feed **Resonance** in some passives. |
| **Tremor / pulse effects** | Field and pulse skills (e.g. **Anchored Tremor**, **Shaking Ground**) build on the same “stone matters” and shared-radius vocabulary. |

## Resources

### Resonance (`resourceId: 'resonance'`)

- **Role**: Earth’s **combat resource** (separate from campaign “elements” in the lobby). Spent on some actives; generated by passives and events.
- **Numbers**: **Max** and **gain chunk sizes** live in `earthCoreConstants.ts` (single source of truth for cards and `Resonance` resource class).
- **How it works in code**: `resources/Resonance.ts` subscribes to engine events, e.g. **round start** (first round onwards), **nearby stone damaged** (only when **self or ally** caused the damage), and **Impact Conversion**’s hook when **armour is removed by incoming damage**. **Fault Harvest** adds Resonance on **your armour breaking** via its passive wiring.
- **Design vs implementation**: Constants also describe **per-second gain while standing on stone** and other pacing knobs; not every constant may be wired into ticks yet—check `Resonance` and engine hooks before assuming a trigger exists.

### Stone (terrain as positioning + triggers)

Stone is not a numeric UI resource, but it **gates strength** and **feeds Resonance** through **damage to nearby stone** events. Creating, cracking, and consuming rock is part of the kit’s **economy of space**.

## How to create or change Earth Core skills

1. **Read this file** when touching any **`05xx`** card, shared Earth constants, or Earth-related engine hooks.
2. **Ids**: New cards use `05` + two-digit index; register the ability in `abilities/AbilityRegistry.ts` and the card in `card_defs/index.ts` (see `card_defs/SKILL.md`).
3. **Tuning shared by the whole tree**: prefer adding or reusing entries in `05_earth_core/earthCoreConstants.ts` instead of magic numbers in a single ability file.
4. **Shared behaviour**: put reusable logic in `abilities/earthCoreHelpers.ts`, `abilities/earthCoreArmour.ts`, `abilities/earthCoreMeleePassives.ts`, or the engine/terrain layer—not copy-pasted across `05_earth_core` folders.
5. **Co-locate** the card under `05_earth_core/####_Name/####Ability.ts`. Optional shared snippets used only by Earth cards can live under e.g. `05_earth_core/0527_EarthCoreShared/`.
6. **Tests**: add or extend tests next to the ability or under existing Earth test files; use the **ability-tests** skill when adding scenario coverage.

## Where Earth Core diverges from common patterns

Document new quirks here as they appear.

- **Two armour helper surfaces**: `abilities/earthCoreArmour.ts` implements **per-source** `sourceMax` and **diminishing** gains for the design in `Plans/plan.md`. `05_earth_core/0527_EarthCoreShared/earthCoreArmour.ts` is a **simpler pool** (`get` / `add` / `spend`) used by some cards (e.g. **Seismic Guard**, **Stoney Punch**, **Fault Harvest** reads). **Do not mix** the two on the same unit for the same effect without checking stack rules—pick the helper that matches the design of the source.
- **Resonance is event-driven**: gains are not always visible in the ability’s `doCardEffect`; they may live in `Resonance`, `GameEngine`, or `abilityEvents` / passives.
- **Stonephase and terrain** need changes in **projectile** and **terrain** code, not only in the card def.
- **Utility group `06`**: cross-theme tools live under `card_defs/utility/`, not in this folder—only **05** Earth kit belongs here.
