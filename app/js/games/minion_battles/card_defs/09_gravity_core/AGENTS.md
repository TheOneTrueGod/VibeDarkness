# Gravity Core

Gravity skill cards (group `09`). Design, the ability spine and Push/Pull rules are in `GravityCore.md`.

## Flavour

The Gravity Core player feels the pull between everything on the field, and learns to grip it: drawing enemies in, throwing them aside, and turning their own weight against them. The near-misses of passing blades and arrows feed that grip.

- Talk about the pull between things, weight, the ground's hold, leaning and falling. Keep it low tech: no scientific words (mass, force, orbit, void, space, gravity fields).
- Write in the player's voice ("You…"), as the Armory does.
- Copy rules are in `.claude/skills/narrative/writing-style-abilities/STYLE.md`.
