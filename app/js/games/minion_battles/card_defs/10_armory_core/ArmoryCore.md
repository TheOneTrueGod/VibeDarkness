# Armory Core

Group 10. A conjured weapon replaces its Create card with one card that toggles between the held strike and the special attack. Equipped weapon abilities stay. Dropped weapons stay on the ground with no expiry. Turrets and repair posts are deployables (field objects), not units. The token exists on the frame it is created and stays hidden until its flight finishes; see `AGENTS.md`.

Create Sword (`1001`) spends Charge and becomes Charge Sword (`1002`), which toggles to Throw Sword (`1003`). Deploy Turret (`1004`) places a turret that shoots down a cone. Repair Post (`1011`) places a post that heals nearby allies each quarter round. Create Mace (`1005`) spends Charge and becomes Charge Mace (`1006`), which toggles to Whirl (`1007`). Create Shield (`1008`) spends Charge and blocks for three seconds. The spinning bucklers last one second, and the card becomes Throw Shield (`1010`), which toggles to Burst (`1009`), at that moment. It starts on Throw Shield. At one and a half seconds another ability ends the rest of the block, and a pass keeps it up. The first block gains Charge. Charge is the shared resource.

The Create abilities are cheap (1 Charge, two uses a round) and put weapon tokens on the ground: a spent or discarded weapon drops one. Throw Sword, Whirl and Release Charge each cost 1 Charge and have **Echo** (`abilities/echo/`): they also happen at each of the caster's own tokens of their weapon (sword, mace, buckler), which are destroyed. Throw Sword throws a copy from each sword in the same direction; Whirl whirls around each mace; Release Charge bursts in a smaller circle around each buckler. Echo copies leave no tokens.

The abilities are archetype configs (`defineConjure`, `defineThrownWeapon`, `defineDirectionalShield`, `defineConeStrike`) over engine-owned weapon and deployable definitions, registered by `registerArmoryContent()`. Colours come from the `armory` theme (`themes/AGENTS.md`). See `AGENTS.md` for the file map.
